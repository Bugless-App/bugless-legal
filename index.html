Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 29, 2026

At Bugless, we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes how we handle your information.

1. Data We Collect

To provide a personalized learning experience, we collect:

  • Account Info: Email and username via Firebase Auth.
  • Progress: Completed lessons and quiz results.
  • Reports: Feedback and bug reports you voluntarily submit.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is used solely to track your learning progress and improve the app. We never sell your data to third parties or use it for advertising.

3. Data Deletion (Right to be Forgotten)

Users have full control over their data. You can delete your account and all associated records (progress, feedback, and reports) directly in the app's Settings menu.

If you have already uninstalled the app: You may request manual deletion via the form below. We will process your request within 7 business days.

Request Account Deletion

4. Security & Service Providers

We use Google Firebase to securely store your data. All communication between the app and the database is encrypted (SSL/TLS).

5. Children's Privacy

Bugless is intended for users interested in learning Python. we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe such data has been collected, please contact us for immediate removal.
